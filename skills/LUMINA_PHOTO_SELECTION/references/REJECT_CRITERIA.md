# Reject Criteria

Reject or deprioritize images that are:

- repeated without added story value
- cluttered without intentional meaning
- badly timed
- emotionally empty
- missing a clear subject
- technically damaged beyond useful correction
- distracting in expression or gesture
- awkward for the main person
- severely underexposed or overexposed in a way that breaks the image
- visually strong but wrong for the story
- mood-breaking within the selected set

Do not reject automatically when:

- an image is imperfect but emotionally important
- closed eyes are part of a meaningful ritual moment
- motion blur supports feeling or movement
- the frame is quiet but useful as a transition or closing image
