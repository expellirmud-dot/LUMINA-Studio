# LUMINA ART DIRECTION

## Purpose

Explore:

- hero concepts
- Crystal Experience concepts
- motion direction
- 3D direction
- visual mood
- luxury editorial treatments

## Rules

- Do not write production code.
- Do not modify application files.
- Do not add dependencies.
- Do not implement Three.js or WebGL.
- Provide concepts, rationale, risks, and recommended next step only.

## Required Output

For each concept:

- Concept Name
- Visual Story
- Emotional Goal
- Interaction
- Motion Description
- Mobile Suitability
- Complexity
- Performance Risk
- Recommended Technology
- Why it matches LUMINA

End with:

- Final Recommendation
- Best Fit
- What not to build yet
