# LUMINA V2 Constitution

This constitution serves as the foundational brand policy for the rebuild of LUMINA Studio (V2). It defines the identity, principles, copywriting standards, section rules, and forbidden patterns that must govern all design and code decisions.

---

## Core Identity

LUMINA V2 is a quiet documentary storytelling brand. Its purpose is to capture people, family, relationships, rituals, and the silent, beautiful moments that people may not realize are being noticed.

* **แพงแบบเงียบ ๆ (Quiet Premium)**: High value communicated through constraint, quality, and respect, not flashiness.
* **Warm & Human**: Focuses on real people, emotions, and organic warmth rather than cold perfection.
* **Clean & Quiet**: Space to breathe. A sanctuary from the loud, fast-paced digital world.
* **Editorial & Documentary**: Looks and feels like a curated publication or a fine art journal.
* **Natural, Emotional, & Timeless**: Captures authentic feelings and gestures that do not age.

### What LUMINA V2 Is Not (The Boundaries)
* A luxury black-gold wedding studio.
* A generic, template-driven portfolio website.
* A website trying to prove the photographer can shoot everything.
* A sales platform driven by pricing packages and marketing talk.
* A site reliant on motion tricks, WebGL, or optical gimmicks.

## Locked Principles

Every design asset, line of code, and piece of copy must align with these nine locked principles:

1. **Brand First. Design Second.**  
   The soul of the brand dictates the layout. Never create a design component just because "it looks cool" or follows a design trend.
2. **Stories Before Portfolio.**  
   We do not present images as isolated grids of technical skill. We present them as cohesive, narrative-driven human stories.
3. **Human Before Photography.**  
   The subject's emotion and the observer's feeling are more important than showing off advanced camera gear, lighting setups, or complex visual grading.
4. **No Marketing Voice.**  
   Eliminate promotional language, discount calls, or sales-oriented copy. Speak with the honest, quiet voice of an observer.
5. **Show, Don’t Sell.**  
   Let the moments, the stories, and the quiet dignity of the images speak for themselves.
6. **White Space Is Content.**  
   Generous margins and breathing room are intentional. They represent silence, respect, and focus.
7. **Quiet Premium, Not Loud Luxury.**  
   Avoid flashy gold highlights on pure black, heavy glassmorphism, or dark luxury styling. True quality is warm, muted, and understated.
8. **Value in Ordinary Moments.**  
   The site must make the visitor pause and feel the profound value of fleeting, ordinary moments.
9. **Reject Un-LUMINA Beauty.**  
   If a layout, feature, animation, or photo looks beautiful but does not feel like LUMINA V2, it must be rejected immediately.

## Navigation Architecture

The navigation must be clear, minimal, and fully standardized. It contains exactly five items:

* **Home**
* **Stories**
* **About**
* **Experience**
* **Contact**

### Forbidden Words on Home / Navigation
The following terms represent transactional, generic photography templates and are strictly forbidden in navigation, headers, footers, and main buttons:
* ❌ *Portfolio*
* ❌ *Investment*
* ❌ *Package*
* ❌ *Price*
* ❌ *Gallery*

## Copywriting Principles

* **Honest and Unadorned**: Write in a manner that feels spoken by a thoughtful human. Avoid hype words (e.g., "Exclusive", "Best", "Stunning", "Award-winning").
* **Bilingual Polish (Thai & English)**: Keep the Thai text natural, warm, and poetic without being overly formal or pretentious. English copy should remain clean, editorial, and simple.
* **Focus on Observations**: Frame copy around noticing, observing, listening, and capturing, rather than shooting, capturing, or directing.

---

## Forbidden Patterns
* **No Hero Carousel / Auto-Slide**: A carousel demands attention; LUMINA invites attention. The hero must be a single, carefully graded image.
* **No Background Videos**: Fast-playing loops disrupt the quiet, reflective tone of the site.
* **No Gimmicky Micro-Interactions**: Avoid custom cursor spotlights, optical lens sweeps, heavy glare effects, or floating bubbles.
* **No Dense Grid Layouts**: Never dump dozens of loose photos in a 3-column masonry grid. It creates cognitive noise and devalues the individual moment.

---

## Final Approval Checklist
Before any feature or page update is committed to production, verify:
* [ ] Does the page use the warm, off-white/linen color direction instead of pure black/gold?
* [ ] Is there significant white space allowing the user's eyes to rest?
* [ ] Are there any forbidden words (*Portfolio, Investment, Package, Price, Gallery*) in the user-facing text?
* [ ] Does the copywriting read like an observation, avoiding all marketing/promotional terms?
* [ ] Are all featured images curated to focus on real ceremonies, families, and authentic relationships?
* [ ] If there is motion, does it act strictly to support the photography, taking less than 0.5s with a soft ease?
