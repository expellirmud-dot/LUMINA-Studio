# LUMINA V2 Visual Language

This document establishes the precise guidelines for image selection, color palettes, typography, motion, and visual curation for the LUMINA Studio V2 rebuild.

---

## Image Selection Rules

All photography must serve the primary rule: **"Design from how Toto sees people."** 

### Image Curation for the Home Page

#### Primary Priority (Core Visuals)
These images must form the heart of the Home page. They represent authentic human connections, family bonds, and the quiet beauty of real life:
* **Real ceremony & rituals**: Unposed moments of connection, respect, and tradition.
* **Family & relationships**: Spontaneous laughter, comfort, and quiet closeness.
* **Hands & gestures**: Whispering, holding hands, blessing, wiping a tear.
* **Generations**: Interactions between children, parents, and grandparents.
* **Moments between planned events**: The transitions, preparation, deep breaths, and quiet breaks.

#### Secondary Priority (Supporting Visuals)
Used sparingly to provide visual breaks or balance:
* **Quiet couple portraits**: Authentic, relaxed, and atmospheric couple shots (e.g., beach pre-wedding with natural light).
* **Clean emotional portraits**: Simple framing focusing on genuine facial expressions.
* **Detail shots**: Close-ups of flowers, light hitting a wooden table, or fabrics, used as editorial visual pauses.

#### Forbidden Home Page Images (Do Not Use)
These images distract from the core human storytelling and must stay outside the Home page:
* ❌ **Stage/Concert/Party Lights**: Neon flashes, stage setups, or intense party environments.
* ❌ **Star/Moon/Long Exposures**: Night skies or high-contrast, technically complex astro-photography.
* ❌ **Motorcycle/Dark Action Portraits**: Aggressive, styling-heavy, or action-themed photos.
* ❌ **Fashion-Heavy Graduation Images**: Highly posed, costume-like, or overly polished fashion graduation shots.
* ❌ **Technical-Skill-First Images**: Photos shot primarily to demonstrate lighting gear, macro lenses, or camera limits, without human emotion.
* ❌ **"Look at My Photography" Images**: Any photo that highlights the photographer's presence or gear over the subject's experience.

## Hero Image rules & grading

The Hero image is the first point of contact with LUMINA. It must not be a generic portfolio image pasted onto the page. It requires a dedicated **Hero Grade** to ensure readability and tone consistency.

### Hero Image Grade Guidelines
* **Slightly Lower Saturation**: Subtle, rich, lifelike tones. No hyper-vivid colors.
* **Soft Contrast**: Avoid harsh black-and-white points. Shadows should feel open, and highlights should roll off gently.
* **Gentle Highlight Rolloff**: Preserves detail in light areas (like a white bridal gown or direct sunlight).
* **Warm, Not Yellow**: A clean, organic warmth resembling natural morning light, avoiding heavy amber filters.
* **Quiet Shadows**: Natural shadows that blend smoothly with text without looking muddy.
* **Text-Readable Overlay**: A soft, linear gradient overlay that ensures the overlaying white/dark text is fully readable without making the background photo look unnaturally darkened.

## Color Palette

The palette is warm, organic, and editorial. We strictly avoid the cold black-and-gold luxury direction.

| Token | Hex Code | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Background** | `#F4F1EB` | The primary linen/warm off-white background of the website. |
| **Surface** | `#FBF9F5` | Lighter cards, panels, and inner container surfaces. |
| **Text** | `#2D2A26` | Deep charcoal for readable, high-contrast headings and body copy. |
| **Muted** | `#7D756E` | Warm gray for secondary copy, dates, and subtle notes. |
| **Accent** | `#A78C78` | Soft clay/warm earth tone for subtle highlights and active states. |
| **Border** | `#E7E1D8` | Soft gray-linen for dividing lines, frames, and borders. |

## Typography Direction

* **Headings**: Editorial serif or warm looped sans-serif.
  * *English*: Clean editorial serif (e.g., Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, or Outfit/Inter for a modern layout).
  * *Thai*: Warm, clean looped typeface (e.g., IBM Plex Sans Thai Looped) to ensure a human and friendly but highly premium editorial tone.
* **Body Copy**: High-readability sans-serif (e.g., Inter, IBM Plex Sans Thai).
* **Scale**: High contrast in sizes. Large, elegant titles surrounded by generous white space, balanced by very small, precise labels and captions.

---

## Motion Direction

Motion must never compete with photography. It must feel like the physical turning of a high-quality paper journal.
* **Fade-in transitions**: 0.4s to 0.6s opacity transitions with soft cubic-bezier easing.
* **Scroll-reveals**: Subtle shifts (e.g., Y-offset of 10px to 20px max) as elements enter the viewport.
* **Hover effects**: Soft transition of border opacity or image scale (maximum 1.02x zoom over 0.5s).
* **No parallax/WebGL**: Avoid heavy cursor spotlights, scrolling speed manipulations, and complex WebGL effects.
