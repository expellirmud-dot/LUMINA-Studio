# LUMINA-QA-013

## Name

Portfolio Curation Audit

## Objective

Evaluate all currently selected portfolio images.

Focus on:

* emotional impact
* storytelling
* luxury perception
* editorial consistency

Do not propose animations.

Do not propose Three.js.

Do not propose WebGL.

Do not review code.

Review photography selection only.

## Deliverables

For each portfolio image:

* Score (1-10)
* Strengths
* Weaknesses
* Keep / Replace

Rank:

* Hero Candidate
* Portfolio Candidate
* About Studio Candidate

Final Output:

Top 5 strongest images currently used on the website.
