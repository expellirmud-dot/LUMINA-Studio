# Selection Principles

LUMINA photo selection is editorial, not decorative.

Select images that:

1. Make the viewer stop.
2. Tell the story of one real event.
3. Contain human presence, emotion, or meaningful timing.
4. Have a clear subject.
5. Add rhythm to the set.
6. Support a specific website or story role.
7. Carry memory, not just surface polish.

Avoid images that:

1. Are beautiful but have no job in the story.
2. Repeat a stronger selected image.
3. Feel disconnected from the event.
4. Look technically clean but emotionally empty.
5. Need layout or effects to become interesting.

When choosing between similar images, prefer:

1. stronger expression
2. cleaner subject attention
3. better timing
4. more meaningful gesture
5. less distracting background
6. stronger fit with the story order
